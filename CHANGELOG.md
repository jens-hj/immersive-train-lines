# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Initial release: Create railways drawn onto the Hoofprint world map and the Immersive Minimaps minimap, pixel for pixel in map colours.
- Two line styles: **Ink** (paper-map railway symbol; tunnels dashed, bridges cased) and **Rails** (track seen from above; tunnels only in cave mode).
- Railways and stations only appear on ground your map has charted.
- Stations are marked and named on hover; trains show when they are near you or standing at a charted station.
